## Primitives

All **46** primitives in `src/app/components/ui/` are documented under
**Primitives** in the sidebar — form controls, overlays, navigation, data
display.

Two things to know before reaching for one:

**Only 7 are actually used in NXUS** — `button`, `input`, `select`, `checkbox`,
`tooltip`, `switch`, `sonner`. The other 39 are stock shadcn scaffolding that
shipped with the Figma Make export; nothing in the app imports them. They are
documented so you know what exists, not to suggest they are load-bearing.

**Where a primitive and the style guide disagree, the style guide wins.** The
shadcn button ships `rounded-md` variants; NXUS buttons are `rounded-full`
(**L-R1**) in the three shapes of **P-CO1**. Each story flags this where it
applies.

`Primitives/Sidebar` is a **stale duplicate** — not shadcn's sidebar, but an old
copy of the NXUS one. Use `src/app/components/Sidebar.tsx`.
